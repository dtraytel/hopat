\begin{abstract}
In this thesis, we work towards formally proving the correctness of Nipkow's algorithm for higher-order pattern unification. The formalization is carried out in the Isabelle/HOL proof assistant. We closely follow the pen-and-paper proofs by Grobauer and add many missing details. We discuss how we extended some of the proofs to make them work in Isabelle. In particular, we show that some of Grobauer's results do not hold for untyped terms. We also show how this extends to the unification algorithm. The Isabelle formalization includes proofs of most of the auxiliary lemmas used by Grobauer and a first step towards proving termination of the unification algorithm. Proving the correctness of the unification algorithm remains to be done.
\end{abstract}
